About The Platform

Built to Solve Real Campus Challenges

Campus facilities degrade silently: broken projectors delay lectures, leaking air conditioning damages desks, and stuffy air impairs learning. AERIS fixes this with transparency and speed.

📡

Real-Time Environmental Telemetry

Monitors Temperature, Humidity, CO2 levels, and Occupancy across campus study halls and lecture rooms to ensure healthy learning conditions.

⚡

QR-Based Instant Reporting

Every room has a physical QR code. Anyone in the room can scan it and file a categorized report with photo evidence in under 30 seconds.

🤝

Crowdsourced Verification

Inspired by smart mapping systems: when another student enters a room, AERIS prompts them to verify whether a reported problem is still active.

User Workflow

How AERIS Works In 5 Simple Steps

From finding an issue to official resolution: a frictionless experience for students and staff.

1

Scan Room QR

Locate the physical QR code placard at any room entrance or desk and scan with your camera.

2

Access Live Status

View the room's real-time air quality, comfort score, active equipment list, and current health.

3

Report An Issue

Select relevant issue category, attach optional photo evidence, specify urgency, and submit.

4

Crowd Verification

Other room visitors confirm if the issue persists, giving facility managers verified consensus.

5

Resolution & Log

Admins dispatch technicians, mark progress, and close the issue into the room's permanent history.
